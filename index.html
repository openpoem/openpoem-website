I build an iPhone app for the 3%.

Not for the likes. Not for the masses. Just for the few.

Those who listen between the words.

To find the truth behind words.

scroll

For those that speak
without words

For those that know
silence kills

For those that see
where it's dark

For those that cry
in the rain

For those that breathe
until it is done

The app

Give it language.
Read what is underneath.

Paste a message, a page, a whole meeting — or speak it — and read what is wanted, what stays unsaid, where the tension sits, and the way back to before.

The Brand

Apple — the word. Before the apple, a word pointed at the thing; after it, at good and evil. The name stepped in front of what it named.

Snake — older than the name. It coils the fruit because meaning was here first, and still knows the way back to before.

Pulse — the frequency beneath the words: the heartbeat under every symbol. Listen there, between the words, and you hear what was meant.

The word is not the thing.
