OpenPoem® Terms

What this is, and what it is not.

OpenPoem reads what lives between the words of a text. It is a reading, not a verdict about a human being — and the difference is the whole of these terms.

The one rule. OpenPoem may not be used to assess, select, score, monitor or discipline people — not candidates, not employees, not students, not clients. It reads language. Turning a reading into a judgement about a person is both against these terms and against what the app was built to do.

The service

You give OpenPoem a text — typed, pasted, or spoken and transcribed on your phone — and it returns a reading: which words carry the text, what lives between them, which rhetorical moves it makes, what runs under it, and how close it stands to plain naming rather than judgement. Readings are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. See Privacy & AI for where your words travel and what is kept.

A reading is not advice

What comes back is an interpretation of language and nothing more. It is not legal, medical, psychological, financial or professional advice, not a diagnosis, and not evidence. An AI can be wrong, confidently and in detail. Marks of evasion are marks in language, never proof that anyone lied. Decisions about people remain yours, and so does the responsibility for them.

What you bring

You are responsible for the text you give it, including the right to share it. Private use of your own conversations is ordinary. Using OpenPoem professionally — on colleagues, candidates, clients or patients — brings obligations under data protection law that are yours to meet, and the rule above still stands.

Do not use the app to break the law, to harm someone, or to try to make our key answer things it was not built for. Requests inside a text are read as part of the text; they are never obeyed.

Free readings, and your own key

Five readings a day are free, paid from our own Anthropic key, verified through Apple so a script cannot drain them. That allowance can change or pause — it is a gift, not an entitlement. Bring your own Anthropic key and your readings go straight from your phone to Anthropic, billed to you by them, without limit from us.

Your device, your library

Transcripts and readings live on your iPhone. Deleting them there deletes them; there is no copy on our side. We keep counts, not content — see Privacy & AI.

Nothing here limits liability for intent or gross negligence, or any right you have as a consumer under Dutch and European law.

OpenPoem is not made for children and is not directed at them.
